Future of Food: 🥒🌱
The future of food depends on how we treat our planet today.
 My Vision:
Climate change is hurting farmers across Africa and the world. 
We need to support farmers with technology, protect healthy soil, 
and reduce food waste to feed the next generation.
The Problem-
1. Climate change is causing droughts and poor harvests.
2. Soil health is declining due to overuse.
3. 1/3 of food produced is wasted globally.
My Solution
This project shares ideas on:
1. Tech for Farmers:Using simple apps and data to help farmers grow more.
2. Healthy Soil: Sustainable  to keep the land fertile. 
3. Zero Waste: Better storage and distribution to reduce food loss.
